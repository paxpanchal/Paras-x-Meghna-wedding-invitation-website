PARAS & MEGHNA WEDDING INVITATION - BACKGROUND MUSIC
=====================================================

This directory houses the background music track for Paras & Meghna's wedding website.

Current file:
/public/music/wedding-theme.mp3 (48-second seamless looping instrumental flute & strings melody)

How to replace with your own royalty-free track:
1. Place any clean MP3 audio file of your choice in this directory.
2. Name it "wedding-theme.mp3" (or update the audioSrc in src/data/weddingData.ts).
3. The centralized audio controller will automatically stream your audio file with seamless native looping and gentle 25% background volume upon guests clicking "OPEN INVITATION".
